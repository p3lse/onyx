ABOUT ONYX

My name is Kareem, but most people know me as Onyx. I’m 16 years old and I create gaming graphics, including esports posters, revamps, banners, and other designs for the gaming community. I enjoy making clean and creative graphics that stand out and match the style people are looking for. I’m always trying to improve my skills, learn new things, and take my designs to the next level. My goal is to become one of the best graphic designers in the gaming and esports community and build a name that people recognize and respect.
